Frequently Asked Questions

The Bowen Technique is a dynamic remedial deep-muscle therapy that releases stress at a deep level, restoring the body's natural state of balance (homeostasis). It works by stimulating the body's own healing mechanisms through gentle, precise rolling movements over muscles, tendons, and ligaments.

Your initial consultation involves a thorough discussion of your medical history, current medications, and the reasons you're seeking treatment. I'll carry out an overall body assessment to understand the full picture before we begin any hands-on work. The initial session typically lasts up to 1 hour.

Treatment takes place on a massage table, though some techniques can be performed standing or sitting. You may undress to your underwear or remain clothed — whichever you prefer. Using thumbs and fingers on precise points, I perform gentle rolling-type moves over muscles, tendons, and ligaments, with rest periods between sets of moves to allow your body to respond. There is no manipulation or force involved. A typical session lasts around 45 minutes, with the initial session lasting up to 1 hour.

While Bowen does not directly "treat" specific conditions, a wide range of conditions respond positively to Bowen therapy. These include back pain, neck and shoulder problems, knee issues, sports injuries, RSI, fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue, migraines, respiratory conditions, and many more. Each person responds individually, and results can vary.

Many conditions are resolved within 2–3 sessions. More chronic or long-standing issues may require additional treatments. Everyone responds differently, and I'll discuss expected timelines with you during your initial consultation. A minimum of 2 initial treatments is recommended to give the therapy the best chance of working effectively.
