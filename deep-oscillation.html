About This Treatment

What Is Deep Oscillation?

Deep Oscillation is a gentle, non-invasive therapy that uses soothing electrostatic vibrations to reach deep into your tissues. Unlike conventional treatments, it does not overwhelm sensitive or painful areas — making it ideal for people living with chronic pain, fibromyalgia, or recovering from surgery.

The treatment can be used very early in the recovery process, often before other manual therapies are appropriate. It is clinically proven electrostatic lymphatic drainage that permeates up to 8cm deep without applying any mechanical pressure to the body.

Clinical Details

Clinically Proven Technology

Deep Oscillation is a CE marked medical device, manufactured to EN ISO 13485 standards. It delivers clinically proven electrostatic lymphatic drainage that permeates up to 8cm into tissue depth — all without applying mechanical pressure to the body.

This makes it particularly effective for conditions where conventional hands-on therapy would be too painful or not yet appropriate, such as immediately following surgery, during acute flare-ups of chronic conditions, or when treating highly sensitive areas.

Reaches 8cm Deep — Without Pressure

Unlike conventional manual therapies, Deep Oscillation reaches up to 8cm into your tissues using gentle electrostatic vibrations. There is no mechanical pressure involved, making it safe and comfortable even for the most sensitive conditions, fresh surgical wounds, and acute injuries.
