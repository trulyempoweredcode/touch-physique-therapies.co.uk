Sports Massage

Targeted, no-nonsense treatment that stretches tight muscles, breaks down adhesions, and keeps your body performing at its best — whether you're a weekend runner or an elite competitor.

About This Treatment

What Is Sports Massage?

Sports massage is a no-nonsense approach to bodywork that stretches tight muscles, stimulates inactive muscles, and improves overall soft tissue condition. It is designed for anyone who is physically active — not just professional athletes.

The treatment specifically addresses adhesions (bands of scar tissue) that can block circulation and cause pain, limited strength, and restricted movement. By breaking these down and restoring healthy tissue function, sports massage helps you move better, recover faster, and perform at your peak.
