Recommended Additional Pages

Based on competitor analysis and best practice for therapy websites, you may want to consider adding these pages in future:

"What to Expect" Page Recommended

A dedicated page walking first-time visitors through what happens before, during, and after a treatment session. This reduces anxiety about booking and is a common pattern on top-performing therapy websites. Your FAQs cover some of this, but a dedicated page with photos would be more reassuring.

"Conditions I Treat" Page Recommended

A single page listing all conditions you treat across all therapies. This is excellent for SEO (people search for conditions, not treatment names) and helps visitors who aren't sure which therapy they need. Could link to the relevant treatment page for each condition.

Blog To Query

A blog would help with ongoing SEO and position you as an authority. Topics could include: treatment explanations, recovery tips, client success stories (with permission), wellness advice. Your AI Site Editor has a built-in blog system you can use. Would you like us to set this up?
