Advanced Deep Tissue Massage

Specialised clinical techniques that go beyond traditional deep tissue to find and fix the root cause of your pain.

More Than a Standard Massage

What Makes This Different

This goes beyond traditional deep tissue massage. Using refined techniques and clinical strategies developed over nearly 20 years of practice, each treatment is tailored specifically to your presenting problems rather than following a generic routine.

Specialised techniques and tools are used to penetrate deeper into muscles and fascia, working to identify and address the root cause of your discomfort rather than simply treating the area that hurts.

Clinical Techniques

Specialised Methods for Deeper Results

A range of advanced techniques are combined within each session depending on what your body needs.

Deep Slow Strokes & Stripping

Using knuckles, forearm, elbows, and thumbs to work through layers of muscle tissue with controlled, deliberate pressure.

Trigger Point Therapy

Targeted pressure on specific points of tension that refer pain to other areas of the body, releasing knots and restoring normal function.

Myofascial Release

Sustained pressure applied to the fascial connective tissue to eliminate pain, restore motion, and release structural restrictions.

Chinese Fire Cupping

Traditional cupping therapy that increases blood flow, loosens fascia, and draws out deep-seated tension from muscles and connective tissue.

MET & Muscle Testing

Muscle Energy Technique and applied kinesiology to identify imbalances, correct pelvic and postural alignment, and restore proper movement patterns.

Acupressure & Tui Na

Traditional Chinese bodywork techniques applying pressure to specific points and meridians to release tension, improve energy flow, and support recovery.

Is This Right for You?

Who Benefits Most

Advanced deep tissue massage is particularly effective for the following conditions and situations.

  • Persistent muscle pain that won't resolve
  • Restricted movement and stiffness
  • Chronic tension and muscle tightness
  • Scar tissue and adhesions
  • Postural issues and repetitive strain
  • Athletic recovery and performance
  • Sports injuries and rehabilitation
  • Work-related pain and occupational strain

Treating the Root Cause

Symptom-massaging can be soothing, but it's like painting over rust. Every session is designed to work beyond the surface, investigating why the pain or restriction is there in the first place and addressing the underlying cause for lasting results.

After treatment you may feel rejuvenated or a bit sore — this is a normal reaction as your body responds to the deep work. Any soreness typically subsides within 24–72 hours.

Medical note: If you have certain conditions such as osteoporosis, clotting disorders, or recent fractures, please consult your healthcare provider before booking.
