How I Can Help

Treatments Tailored to Your Body

Every person's experience of pain and tension is unique. I offer six specialist therapies that can be combined to support your recovery and wellbeing.

Bowen Therapy NST

A gentle yet powerful bodywork technique that activates your body's innate healing abilities. Over 80% of clients are symptom-free within 1–3 sessions.

Learn more

Deep Oscillation

Gentle, non-invasive therapy that reaches 8cm deep into tissue without pressure — ideal for post-surgery recovery, chronic pain, and fibromyalgia.

Learn more

Sports Massage

Targeted treatment to stretch tight muscles, break down adhesions, and support athletic performance — from weekend runners to elite competitors.

Learn more

Deep Tissue Massage

Advanced clinical techniques including trigger point therapy, myofascial release, and Chinese fire cupping — working to find and fix the root cause, not just mask symptoms.

Learn more

Bowen & Fascial Unwinding

A unique combination of Bowen Therapy and fascial unwinding that calms the nervous system, releases stored tension, and supports trauma recovery.

OldPain2Go

A guided process that works with your brain to switch off pain signals that are no longer needed — particularly effective for chronic pain when medical options have been exhausted.
